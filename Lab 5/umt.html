Welcome to UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It is recognised for its specialisation in marine science, aquatic resources and environmental studies. The university provides various academic programmes in science, technology and management.

About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM), located at Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu. The centre provided facilities for students to conduct practical sessions and supported research activities by lecturers.

In June 1996, the centre was transformed into a branch campus known as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). Later, it became Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) in 1999 and was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was upgraded to full university status and officially renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT). Today, UMT continues to focus on education, research and development, particularly in marine and environmental-related fields.

History of UMT

UMT has experienced several stages of development since its establishment as a fisheries and marine science centre. The following table highlights important milestones in its history.

Historical Development of UMT
Year Institution Name Development
1979 - 1996 Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science Started as a centre under UPM.
1996 - 1999 UPMT Became a branch campus in Terengganu.
1999 - 2001 KUT Establishment of Terengganu University College.
2001 - 2007 KUSTEM Renamed as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia.
2007 - now UMT Upgraded to full university status.

Faculties at UMT

UMT offers various academic programmes through its faculties and academic centres. Some of its academic areas include:

  • Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics (FSKM)
  • Faculty of Marine Science and Environment (FSSM)
  • Faculty of Maritime Studies (FPM)
  • Faculty of Fisheries Science and Aquaculture (FSPA)
  • Faculty of Food Science and Agrotechnology (FSMA)
  • Faculty of Business, Economics and Social Development (FPEPS)
  • Faculty of Ocean Engineering Technology (FTKK)
