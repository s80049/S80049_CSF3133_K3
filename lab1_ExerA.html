How to Use Our Services

  1. Prepare your document in a digital format (PDF, Word, etc.).
  2. Visit the UMT Printing Shop during operating hours.
  3. Submit your document to the staff and specify your printing preferences.
  4. Make payment for the services rendered.
  5. Collect your printed documents once they are ready.
